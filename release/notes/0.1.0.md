### New

- First release: the fault codes of eight control units (engine, second engine computer, gearbox, transfer case, stability control, airbags, steering, body), read and cleared on the iDrive screen or in the phone app.
- Codes are six hex digits, as BMW's tools print them, with their state (active, stored, pending).
- Codes are cleared only when the car is parked.

### Good to know

- Needs NBT Manager 0.4.0 or later.
- On a car where NBT Manager reaches no gateway, no unit answers (seen on a G31). NBT Manager 0.5.5 looks for the gateway by itself.
